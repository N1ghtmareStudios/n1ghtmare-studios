N1ghtmare Studios is a one-person studio based in Sweden. Design, code, art direction and audio all land on the same desk. The through-line is dark fantasy with real systems underneath: simulations you can learn, builds you can break, and choices you can regret. No live-service treadmill, no pay-to-win — you buy the game, you own the game.

Boilerplate

Short version, free to paste at the end of an article.

N1ghtmare Studios is a one-person independent game studio based in Sweden, founded in 2025. It makes dark fantasy games with deep systems and hard choices, including the auto-battler Warpack, the story action RPG Soulsmith and the survivors-like Bonecount. More at n1ghtmarestudios.com.

Coming soon to Steam · Demo at Next Fest in October

Warpack

Auto-battler · Roguelite · Asynchronous PvP · PC

Build a warband from a shop, fit it into a backpack that never has enough room, then send it out to fight the armies other players have left behind. Every battle is a puzzle you solved twenty seconds ago. The backpack is the build: space, adjacency and shape matter as much as what you bought. Four factions to master, and a fifth marching in behind them.

Soulsmith

Story action RPG · 2D · Chapter-gated · PC

A blacksmith's apprentice in a world that stopped making heroes. Forge the weapon, then find out what it costs to carry it. Smithing, fusion and gear that carries the story as much as the stats. The level cap holds until the chapter's boss falls — beat it or stay humble. Launching into Early Access with the opening chapters.

In development

Bonecount

Action roguelite · Survivors-like · Same world as Soulsmith · PC

A twin-stick souls-survivor set in the same world as Soulsmith. The waves come, the bosses fall, and every bone you take home is currency — the fallen become permanent power back at the hub. Classes, perks, relics and skills that stack into something unreasonable.

Bonecount is early and its press kit is not ready yet. Email contact@n1ghtmarestudios.com if you want to cover it and we will sort you out.
