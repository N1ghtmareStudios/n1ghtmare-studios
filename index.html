Warpack

Auto-battler · Roguelite · Asynchronous PvP · PC

Build a warband from a shop, fit it into a backpack that never has enough room, then send it out to fight the armies other players have left behind. Every battle is a puzzle you solved twenty seconds ago.

  • The backpack is the build. Space, adjacency and shape matter as much as what you bought.
  • Fight real warbands. Opponents are ghost armies built by other players. No waiting for a match.
  • Four factions to master, and a fifth marching in behind them.
  • Runs last minutes, not evenings. Lose, learn the counter, go again.

Soulsmith

Story action RPG · 2D · Chapter-gated · PC

A blacksmith's apprentice in a world that stopped making heroes. Forge the weapon, then find out what it costs to carry it.

  • Forge what you fight with. Smithing, fusion and gear that carries the story as much as the stats.
  • Chapter gates, not grind. The level cap holds until the chapter's boss falls. Beat it or stay humble.
  • A hand-built world. Zones, quests, companions and bosses written one chapter at a time.
  • Launching into Early Access with the opening chapters; the saga grows from there.

Bonecount

Action roguelite · Survivors-like · Same world as Soulsmith · PC

A twin-stick souls-survivor set in the same world as Soulsmith. The waves come, the bosses fall, and every bone you take home is currency.

  • Bones are the economy. The fallen become permanent power back at the hub.
  • Build a run, break it. Classes, perks, relics and skills that stack into something unreasonable.
  • Same darkness as Soulsmith. One world, two very different fights.

About

One person, and a very long list.

N1ghtmare Studios is a one-person studio based in Sweden. Design, code, art direction, audio and everything else lands on the same desk, which means the games get made the way they were imagined, and nothing gets watered down in a meeting.

The through-line is dark fantasy with real systems underneath: simulations you can learn, builds you can break, and choices you can regret. No live-service treadmill. No pay-to-win. You buy the game, you own the game.

The Lab

Some things we build just to watch them move.

Tendril is a living Physarum simulation: hundreds of thousands of agents sensing, turning and laying trails, all running on your GPU right now, in this browser. Draw with a mouse or a finger and the colony follows your hand. It began as an experiment on a phone; pieces of it are quietly growing into whatever comes next.
