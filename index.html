Warpack

Auto-battler · Roguelite · Asynchronous PvP · PC

Build a warband from a shop, fit it into a backpack that never has enough room, then send it out to fight the armies other players have left behind. Every battle is a puzzle you solved twenty seconds ago.

  • The backpack is the build. Space, adjacency and shape matter as much as what you bought.
  • Fight real warbands. Opponents are ghost armies built by other players — no waiting for a match.
  • Four factions to master, and a fifth marching in behind them.
  • Runs last minutes, not evenings. Lose, learn the counter, go again.

About

One person, and a very long list.

N1ghtmare Studios is a one-person studio based in Sweden. Design, code, art direction, audio and everything else lands on the same desk — which means the games get made the way they were imagined, and nothing gets watered down in a meeting.

The through-line is dark fantasy with real systems underneath: simulations you can learn, builds you can break, and sessions short enough to fit into a real life. No live-service treadmill. No pay-to-win. You buy the game, you own the game.
